# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (React + Vite + Tailwind CSS + TypeScript)

## Users

Engineering teams, product managers, scrum masters, and tech leads who manage software projects using Agile methodologies (Scrum & Kanban). They value speed, clarity, low cognitive load, and visual precision. They spend hours every day in this tool, so clutter, clunky interactions, or slow UI directly degrades their productivity.

## Product Purpose

Tasko is a high-performance, developer-first Agile project management system that combines the deep issue tracking and Scrum workflows of Jira with the fluid, modern aesthetic and speed of Linear. It empowers teams to track sprints, manage backlog items, visualize Kanban boards, plan timelines, and monitor sprint velocity without the visual heaviness or complexity of legacy enterprise software.

## Operating Context

- Users work across desktop monitors and laptops with rich interactive dashboards (Kanban boards, backlog drag-and-drop, sprint cycles, control boards, timeline views).
- The frontend operates with an authentication flow (JWT + Refresh tokens), role-based workspace hierarchies (Owner, Admin, Member, Viewer), and real-time reactive state.
- Empty states are purposeful, encouraging new users to create their first workspace, project, and task seamlessly without dead ends.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Personality:** Professional, crisp, decisive, agile, and minimal.
- **Three-word personality:** **Precise, swift, refined**.
- **Design Stance against AI Slop:**
  - **No cheesy purple-blue gradient soup** applied aimlessly over cards or buttons.
  - **No card-in-card inception**: cards must not be arbitrarily wrapped inside redundant nested borders or bulky container boxes.
  - **Flat & tactile**: subtle, crisp 1px borders (`border-slate-200/80`), gentle elevation shadows (`shadow-2xs` / `shadow-xs`), soft hover lifts, and purposeful status accents.
  - **Restraint in color**: neutral slate background, clean white cards, with color reserved for semantic states (emerald for completed/done, amber for in-progress/starred, rose for blockers/overdue, indigo for active branding).
  - **Typography hierarchy**: clear scale, consistent weights, and legible data density.
